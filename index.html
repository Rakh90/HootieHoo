Send via

SMS opens a group text to everyone enabled. Email opens a draft bcc'd to everyone with an email address on file — useful for contacts without a phone number, or if group SMS isn't reliable between iPhone and Android in your group.

Notification sound

Not something this app (or any website) can set — SMS and iMessage don't carry a "play this sound" instruction, so the sender has no control over it. Each recipient can give your number a distinct tone themselves: on iPhone, Contacts → your card → Edit → Text Tone. On Android it depends on the Messages app, usually under the contact's notification settings.

Share this app

Send this to whoever's on your list so they know what a HootieHoo text is and where it came from. "Share link" opens your phone's normal share sheet (Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, etc. — whatever's installed); where that's not available, it falls back to a blank text message with the link already typed in, so you just pick who to send it to.

Data

Everything — members, messages, activity log — is stored only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere except the message itself, and only when you tap Send in the app that opens.

Resetting messages only touches the two button titles/texts and the send channel above — your members list and activity log are left alone.
